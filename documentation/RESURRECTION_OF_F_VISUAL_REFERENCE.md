Current scope map:

- Goku Blue, Golden Frieza, fading stamina, Sorbet's shot/collapse and Vegeta's base-form handover are implemented in Blue and Gold. The exact ocean staging, orbital/macro shots, ring close-up, clothing wound, chest stomp and two-finger counter remain refinements.

## Reference A — full film and finale

Here is the full, frame-by-frame visual, camera, and narrative breakdown of ***Dragon Ball Z: Resurrection 'F'***—from its setting on Earth to the explosive climax and post-credits ending.

---

## 1. Setting & Premise

* **Where It Takes Place:** The movie takes place on **Earth** (primarily at North City / desolate coastal mountain plateaus) and on **Beerus' Planet**.
* **Timeline Placement:** It takes place in Age 779, approximately one year after *Battle of Gods*.
* **The Inciting Incident:** Sorbet and Tagoma gather the Dragon Balls on Earth to wish Frieza back to life in pieces. Frieza is reassembled in a medical chamber and trains for four months to achieve a power level beyond Super Saiyan God.

---

## 2. Frieza’s Golden Transformation (Visuals & Camera View)

When Frieza realizes base and Super Saiyan forms are no longer enough, he decides to unveil his true evolution.

```
 [ Wide Shot: Floating Above Sea ] ──> [ Low-Angle Torso Flare ] ──> [ Blinding Golden Column ] ──> [ Close-Up Face Reveal ]

```

* **Shot 1: Low-Angle Wide Shot**
* **Camera Placement:** Placed at sea level looking up as Frieza hovers mid-air over the ocean.
* **Visual:** Frieza draws his arms to his waist, flexing his chest. A deep golden aura ignites beneath his feet, casting bright yellow highlights on the ocean surface below.


* **Shot 2: 360-Degree Orbital Pan (The Power Surge)**
* **Camera Placement:** The camera orbits rapidly around Frieza as his energy expands into a fiery golden pillar.
* **Visual:** The skin on his arms and legs turns a metallic, polished gold hue. His chest plate, shoulders, and head-shield shift into a deep purple glossy finish.


* **Shot 3: Macro Close-Up (Golden Frieza Reveal)**
* **Camera Placement:** Tight, direct frontal close-up on Frieza’s face.
* **Visual:** The blinding golden light condenses into a shimmering aura with floating light particles. Frieza smiles sadistically, opening his hands:
> **Frieza:** *"I admit, it's a bit tacky... but you can call this 'Golden Frieza'!"*

## 3. Goku Gets Ambushed (The Ray Gun Attack)

Although Golden Frieza initially outpowers Super Saiyan Blue Goku, Frieza’s stamina rapidly drains because he rushed to Earth without mastering the form. Goku forces Frieza onto his knees.

```
 [ Goku Standing Over Frieza ] ──> [ Macro Finger Focus (Sorbet) ] ──> [ Laser Pierces Chest ] ──> [ Goku Drops ]

```

* **Shot 1: Eye-Level Medium Shot (Goku Drops His Guard)**
* **Camera Placement:** Placed over Frieza’s shoulder looking up at Goku.
* **Action:** Goku powers down from Super Saiyan Blue to his base form. He stands casually over Frieza, lecturing him about his stamina flaw and telling him to leave Earth.


* **Shot 2: Low Ground Macro Shot (Sorbet's Ring)**
* **Camera Placement:** Extreme close-up on Sorbet hiding behind a rock formation nearby.
* **Action:** Sorbet raises his right index finger, aiming his high-tech **Bad Ring Laser** straight at Goku's back.


* **Shot 3: High-Speed Tracking Beam & Impact**
* **Camera Placement:** Side-profile shot tracking a needle-thin crimson beam traveling through the air.
* **Impact:** Because Goku completely lowered his Ki defense, the laser punches straight through Goku’s chest. Smoke rises from a burning hole in his gi as Goku gasps, collapses to his knees, and falls face-first into the dirt.


* **Shot 4: Frieza's Brutality**
* Frieza laughs hysterically, stepping on Goku’s chest wound and torturing him on the ground.

## 4. Vegeta’s Almost Victory & The Destruction of Earth

Vegeta steps in to save the battered Goku, ordering Krillin to feed Goku a Senzu Bean. Vegeta then powers up into **Super Saiyan Blue**.

```
 [ SSJ Blue Vegeta Stance ] ──> [ Ground Pummeling ] ──> [ Desperate Ground Blast ] ──> [ Earth Core Shatters ]

```

* **Shot 1: Low-Angle Upward Shot (Vegeta Powering Up)**
* **Camera Placement:** Placed on the ground, looking up at Vegeta’s cobalt-blue aura flaring over the battered Frieza.
* **Action:** Vegeta delivers a cold, methodical beatdown. He dodges Frieza's wild strikes with minimal movement and pummels Frieza until he drops out of his Golden form back into his standard final form.


* **Shot 2: The Stance Before the Execution**
* **Camera Placement:** Over-the-shoulder shot from behind Vegeta looking down at Frieza lying on his knees in the crater.
* **Action:** Vegeta raises his palm, charging a lethal Ki blast to finish Frieza off permanently.


* **Shot 3: Frieza’s Last Resort & Destruction of Earth**
* **Camera Placement:** Wide shot showing Frieza’s face slammed against the dirt.
* **Visual:** Realizing he has lost, Frieza roars in pure spite, driving his palm directly into the ground beneath him. He injects a massive wave of Ki straight into Earth's core.
* **Shot 4 (Space View):** Extreme wide shot from Earth's orbit. Jagged red magma cracks burst across the globe before the entire planet explodes into a blinding supernova, killing Vegeta and destroying Earth.

## 5. Restoration of Time (Whis's Temporal Do-Over)

Beerus and Whis shield Goku, Gohan, Piccolo, Tien, Master Roshi, Bulma, and Krillin inside Whis's divine barrier in deep space, making them the sole survivors.

```
 [ Space Shield Bubble ] ──> [ Staff Tap (3-Minute Rewind) ] ──> [ Time Reverses ] ──> [ Earth Re-forms ]

```

* **Shot 1: Close-Up on Goku's Regret**
* **Camera Placement:** Tight shot on Goku clenching his fists inside the barrier, furious at himself for letting his guard down and giving Frieza the opening to destroy Earth.


* **Shot 2: Whis's Temporal Do-Over**
* **Action:** Whis explains he can rewind time by **three minutes**. Whis taps his staff twice on the void of space. A ripple of swirling cyan light spreads outward, reversing the planetary explosion.

* **Shot 2: The God Kamehameha**
* **Camera Placement:** Side-profile tracking shot.

* **Shot 3: Vegeta’s Confusion & Resolution**

* **The Setting:** Earth's Hell—a bright, colorful meadow with stuffed animals, angels, and fairies playing music around a giant tree trunk.

## Reference B — outfits, counter and rewind

Here is the full, frame-by-frame visual, dialogue, and camera breakdown of those iconic moments from ***Dragon Ball Z: Resurrection 'F'***—including Goku and Vegeta's updated clothing, Whis's explanation of time rewind, and Goku's legendary "point-blank counter-punch" technique!

---

## 1. Goku and Vegeta's New Whis-Trained Clothing

When Goku and Vegeta return from training under Whis on Beerus' Planet, they wear brand-new combat gear personally signed by Whis:

* **Goku’s Gi:**
* A redesigned, bright orange Turtle School gi without an undershirt.
* The top overlaps higher on his chest, secured by a navy-blue sash.
* Features Whis’s hand-drawn swirl signature in black ink over the left chest.
* Wears navy-blue wristbands and matching martial arts boots with red trim.


* **Vegeta’s Armor:**
* A modern, dark charcoal-grey bodysuit beneath a newly engineered, sleek white-and-grey battle armor chestplate.
* The armor lacks the traditional hip guards, providing maximum mobility.
* Whis’s swirl signature is drawn on the upper-left collar of his chestplate.
* Wears pure white segmented gloves and pointed white boots with navy toes.



---

## 2. Goku Tanks Frieza’s Punch & The "Finger-Gun" Counter (Frame-by-Frame)

During their brawl in Super Saiyan Blue, Golden Frieza tries to overpower Goku with a desperate, full-force right cross straight to Goku’s jaw. Goku uses his divine Ki to tank the hit effortlessly, resulting in one of the most disrespectful and epic counters in DBZ history.

```
 [ Slow-Mo Jaw Impact ] ──> [ The Unshaken Side-Glare ] ──> [ Two-Finger Gun Placement ] ──> [ Point-Blank Shockwave Blast ]

```

### Shot 1: Slow-Motion Impact (Extreme Close-Up)

* **Camera Placement:** Tight macro shot locked on Goku's left jawline.
* **The Action:** Time slows down significantly. Golden Frieza's massive, metallic gold fist lands directly on Goku's cheek. The shockwave ripples across Goku's skin in super slow-motion, but Goku’s head **does not move even a single millimeter**. His cyan hair doesn't flinch.

### Shot 2: The Slow-Turn Side Glare

* **Camera Placement:** Over-the-shoulder shot from behind Frieza's outstretched arm, looking at Goku's face.
* **The Action:** Goku slowly, calmly rotates his eyes toward Frieza. His glowing cyan pupils lock onto Frieza with a stoic, cold glare. Frieza’s eyes widen in sheer horror as he realizes his strongest punch felt like a feather to Goku.

### Shot 3: The "Finger-Gun" Stance (Tight Frontal Shot)

* **Camera Placement:** Placed low, looking up between Goku and Frieza's torsos.
* **The Visual:** Goku doesn't throw a wild punch. He smoothly raises his right hand and curls his ring and pinky fingers into his palm, extending his index and middle fingers together **"like a gun."**
* **The Placement:** In slow motion, Goku gently presses the tips of those two fingers directly into the center of Frieza’s golden chest plate/solar plexus.

### Shot 4: The Point-Blank Release

* **Camera Placement:** Wide side-profile shot as time snaps back to full speed.
* **The Action:** Goku doesn't just shoot Ki; he uses those two fingers as a focal point to drive a brutal, point-blank kinetic shockwave straight through Frieza’s body. The sound effect snaps with a heavy, bass-boosted crack. Frieza's back bulges outward from the force, and he is sent skimming across the ocean waters at hypersonic speed!

---

## 3. Whis's Explanation of Time & The Temporal Do-Over

After Frieza destroys Earth's core, Whis rescues the Z-Fighters inside his divine floating barrier. Inside the quiet bubble surrounded by cosmic debris, Whis explains his temporal powers.

```
 [ Space Shield Isolation ] ──> [ The 3-Minute Limit Rule ] ──> [ Staff Tap Ripple ] ──> [ Rewinding the Extinction ]

```

### Dialogue & Visual Breakdown:

* **The Scene:** Goku is on his knees inside the glowing blue barrier, punching the energy wall in anger.
> **Goku:** *"Dammit! I had him! I was too soft... I lowered my guard and let him destroy everything!"*
> **Vegeta:** *(Dead in the planet explosion outside the bubble)*
> **Beerus:** *"You see, Son Goku? Your soft heart and overconfidence are your greatest flaws. You had the victory in your hands."*


* **Whis's Rule & Explanation:**
* **Camera Placement:** Close-up on Whis holding his divine staff, his face calm and angelic.
* **The Dialogue:**
> **Whis:** *"It seems I have no choice. I shall grant you a do-over."*
> **Goku:** *"A do-over!?"*
> **Whis:** *"My ability allows me to turn back time, but only by a maximum of **three minutes**. It is a technique I use when Lord Beerus accidentally destroys a star or a planet during a tantrum. But remember, I cannot do this often, as it distorts the natural flow of time."*




* **The Visuals of the Time Rewind:**
* Whis taps the bottom of his ringed staff against the void of space twice: ***TAP. TAP.***
* A brilliant ripple of turquoise and white light spreads across the galaxy.
* The camera tracks in reverse: the shattered planetary debris, magma chunks, and exploding atmosphere of Earth pull back together like a movie played backward. Time rewinds exactly to three minutes prior, placing Goku back on the battlefield right before Frieza touches the ground!
