## 1. Universe 6 — setting, lore, and roster

### Supplied lore and narrative

The supplied narrative describes 12 parallel universes paired as twins whose numbers sum to 13, with Universes 6 and 7 as mirrors. Saiyans, members of Frieza's race, and Namekians appear in both, with divergent histories.

In these notes, Universe 7's Planet Sadala was destroyed by civil war; the Saiyans then conquered Planet Vegeta before Frieza destroyed it. Universe 6 retains Sadala, and its Saiyans act as peacekeepers rather than planetary raiders.

Beerus and Champa's food dispute leads to a five-on-five tournament. Champa discovers the planetary Super Dragon Balls scattered across Universes 6 and 7 and proposes swapping Earths if he wins, so that he can enjoy Earth's food.

These are owner-supplied lore notes. Use the project's approved requirements when deciding what to depict or state in-game.

### Arena opening and roster debut

- Open with a sweeping high-angle pan across a colossal cosmic arena in neutral space between Universes 6 and 7.
- A floating stadium sits against a star-filled void, with planetary-sized Super Dragon Balls hovering silently behind it.
- A medium-wide tracking shot follows Vados leading the Universe 6 team onto the platform.
- Emphasize the contrast between Cabbe, Frost, Hit, Botamo, and Auta Magetta rather than introducing them with interchangeable poses.

## 2. Universe 6 — Cabbe's awakening

The supplied text contains two alternative descriptions. Preserve them as alternatives until one is selected against the approved saga plan.

### Version A — pressure, awakening, and caught punch

1. **Pressure:** A low-angle shot looks up at SSJ Blue Vegeta as he deliberately goads and pressures Cabbe, testing his resolve.
2. **Awakening:** An extreme close-up isolates Cabbe's trembling eyes. The camera rapidly circles him through 360 degrees as a fiery golden aura erupts and his dark hair rises into Super Saiyan spikes.
3. **Lesson:** Vegeta catches Cabbe's enraged punch with a calm smirk. The scene reveals that Vegeta provoked him to teach him how to unlock the transformation for the Saiyans on Sadala.

### Version B — threat to Sadala, tanked blows, and Blue finish

The supplied beat chain is: low-angle stance → threat to Sadala → transformation explosion → Blue-flash lesson. The source calls the transformation beat an “S-Cell Explosion”; do not treat this label as a requirement for a new simulation system.

1. **Arena pan:** A high-angle camera sweeps across the floating ring, deep space, and glowing Super Dragon Balls behind the stadium.
2. **Mentor's provocation:** A low-angle close-up shows Base Vegeta standing over a battered, kneeling Cabbe. Cabbe gasps for air. Vegeta crosses his arms with a cold scowl and threatens to eradicate Sadala if Cabbe surrenders.
3. **Super Saiyan trigger:** A tight close-up captures Cabbe's eyes shrinking in horror and veins rising across his forehead. A jagged golden shockwave cracks the ring around his boots. His hair becomes golden spikes, a roaring yellow aura surrounds him, and he lunges forward screaming with a flurry of punches into Vegeta's chest.
4. **Golden lesson:** Vegeta takes the blows rather than dodging. He flashes into Super Saiyan Blue, illuminating Cabbe's face with cobalt light, then knocks him out with a restrained gut strike and catches him as he falls.

Supplied draft dialogue for the closing lesson:

> Remember this feeling. This is Super Saiyan... never forget it.

### Default draft sequence for this encounter

Use the later, detailed Version B as the draft default unless the approved saga plan establishes a different sequence. Version A remains an alternative reference, not an additional mandatory sequence. Choose a single form progression and choreography: Version A begins with Blue Vegeta and a caught punch; Version B begins with Base Vegeta, tanked blows, and a later Blue finish. Do not start in Blue and also stage the later Blue reveal as the first transformation without intentionally adapting the sequence.

## 4. Future Trunks — lore, character visuals, and ruined Earth

### Supplied Zero Mortals Plan narrative

The supplied notes describe Zamasu as a Universe 10 Supreme Kai apprentice disgusted by mortal violence. Goku's use of divine Ki intensifies that hatred into the Zero Mortals Plan.

In the described alternate timeline, Zamasu kills Gowasu, takes the Time Ring and Super Dragon Balls, swaps bodies with Goku, and kills Goku and his family. As Goku Black, he enters Future Trunks's ruined timeline and recruits Future Zamasu, who obtains immortality through that timeline's Super Dragon Balls. The pair eliminate Supreme Kais across the universes, thereby also killing their corresponding Gods of Destruction, and devastate Future Earth.

These are owner-supplied narrative notes, not independently verified chronology. They do not establish which exposition, missions, or timeline mechanics already exist in the project.
