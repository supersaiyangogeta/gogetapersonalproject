## The owner's recollections

- **"Goku and Vegeta kick and strike Zamasu in succession, with dramatic slow motion."** No source describes a strike-by-strike sequence. The closest verifiable beat is #6 (episode 65): they combine their power to destroy the energy bird, then attack Zamasu directly and **he blocks them easily and knocks them down**. Built as: the bird destroyed in play, then a coordinated team rush (alternating blows from both sides, the spinning kick and the axe kick slowed down) that ends with the god catching the last double blow — slowed too — and throwing them into the rubble. (The Free Battle version, the Saiyan Assault, lands that last double blow instead, also slowed.) **The blows, their order and the slow motion are an adaptation; the outcome (blocked, knocked down) is the source's.**
- **Vegito appearing above, looking down.** Not in the synopses. Kept as the owner's recollection (already labelled in `ft_vegito.js`).
- **"Alright!"** Not in the synopses. Kept untouched.
- **Vegito stopping an attack with two fingers.** From an outside source read in an earlier session; not in the Kanzenshuu synopsis. Kept, labelled.
