## 2. What is missing

1. **Bug — the skill wheel ignores form techniques.** `radial.js:24` reads `f.def.specials`, not `f.specFor(slot)`. When a form replaces a technique, the wheel shows the base move's name, icon and `requiresMech` lock. The HUD cards use `specFor` and are correct (`hud.js:855`). Today this is mostly invisible because the names match, but it breaks as soon as forms get their own ultimates.
2. **No generic form or unlock gate for extras.** `extras[].forms` is only checked inside God Bind's own technique (`techniques.js:1484`). There is no `freeUnlock` for techniques (only for forms).
3. **An extra marked `ultimate: true` is not fully treated as an ultimate.** The wheel blocks it when ultimates are off (`radial.js:50`), but `_trySpecial` only checks `slot === 'ultimate'` (`fighter.js:1175`), and the ultimate counter only counts that slot (`fighter.js:1215`).
4. **Ultimate presentation is thin.** A `cinematic`/`ultimate` beam gets one charge shot of at most 2.2 s (`fighter.js:2583`, `battle.js:985`); the Spirit Bomb also gets a follow shot. There are no release, impact or resolution shots, and a `rush` ultimate (Dragon Fist) gets no camera at all.
5. **The CPU uses extras only where hand-coded** (Gogeta's brain, `controllers.js:2109-2173`). A new extra would be invisible to the AI.
6. **One-shot audio has no priority.** `SL.Audio.play` drops any sound once 40 voices are counted (`audio.js:177`), first come first served, so a finisher can lose to punches. Two likely causes of the "audio goes silent" report, for the later audio task: the counter is incremented before the sound function runs and is never decremented if that function throws (`audio.js:180-182`), and nothing resumes a context that stops running mid-battle. These are hypotheses, not verified.

## 3. Design rules (from the PDF, adapted to this code)

- Several supers/ultimates per character = the **three buttons plus extras on the wheel**. The Ultimate button holds the form's main finisher; other finishers are wheel extras. No loadout screen in the first slice.
- Per-form identity = `form.specials` (replaces a button's move) and `extras[].forms` (adds a wheel move in some forms). A distinct move such as the SSJ4 Ultimate Dragon Fist is its own spec with its own id, not a `damageMul`.
- Presentation = an optional **`cine` timeline on a technique spec**, using the transformation step vocabulary plus a few new shot names. It is played by the same kind of frame-step runner as `transformSequence`, uses `kind: 'gameplay'` so Settings → Cinematic off/reduced still works, and always ends the shot and clears the mood in the state's exit hook (the PDF's safety rule: the camera never keeps control).
- Gates = the existing `lockedSlots` / `teach` for the story; a new `freeUnlock` on techniques for Free Battle, the same rule as forms.

## 4. Proposed first slice (for approval)

Small, Goku-only, every piece reused by later characters.

**A. Engine (shared, small):**
1. `radial.js`: use `f.specFor(slot)` for the three button slots (fixes bug 1).
2. Generic extra gates in one helper used by both the wheel and `_trySpecial`: `forms: [...]` (move God Bind's check there), `freeUnlock` (same rule as `formFreeUnlocked`), and `ultimate: true` respects `rules.allowUltimates` and counts as an ultimate (fixes 2 and 3).
3. `spec.cine`: an optional step timeline, run from `beamCharge` / `beamFire` / `rush` / `spiritBomb` by state frame. New shot names: `charge` (existing `ultimateChargeShot`), `release` (behind the user along the aim), `impact` (wide on the target), `follow` (existing `bombFollowShot`). Safety exit in the state exit hook.

**B. Goku content:**
1. **Spirit Bomb**: give it a `cine` (low three-quarter angle, tilt up as the sphere grows, wide reveal). Keep it as base/Kaioken Goku's Ultimate button.
2. **Instant Transmission Kamehameha**: add a short camera snap behind the opponent when the existing Instant Kamehameha fires. No new move.
3. **Dragon Fist** as a new extra for base Goku (`type: 'rush'`, its own poses and golden dragon trail VFX), `forms: ['ssj3']`, plus a `cine` (tight stance, low-angle rush, side track, wide dragon reveal). It can miss: the existing `rush` approach.
4. **SSJ4 Goku (`goku_ssj4`)**: split the regular Dragon Fist (stays `special2`) from a new **Ultimate Dragon Fist** with its own id, poses, trail and `cine`. Where it goes depends on question 2 below.

**C. Checks:** a small automated test (wheel shows form techniques; `forms` / `freeUnlock` gates; an ultimate extra is blocked when ultimates are off; the `cine` exit restores the camera when the user is hit mid-move), the narrow existing suites that cover specials, and a browser check of each new sequence. The full suite only if the owner authorises it (the change touches shared combat code).

Not in this slice: Vegeta, Gohan, fusions, villains (they reuse A next), a loadout screen, AI for the new extras (the CPU keeps its current moves), the audio fixes (separate task, section 2 item 6).

## 6. Built (2026-10-16)

The owner approved section 4 and answered section 5: (2) SSJ4 Goku stays the separate GT character, Ultimate Dragon Fist goes there; (3) main Goku's Dragon Fist unlocks after `buu_kidbuu`; (4) the Spirit Bomb stays on the Ultimate button in every form.

Built as proposed, with three decisions made while building:
- **Cinematics run outside the story only.** Story set pieces (the Spirit Bomb missions retune the charge with `specOverrides`) stage their own camera; a technique cine there could cut across a scene. Story fights behave exactly as before.
- **Dragon Fist is also `freeOnly`**, so story fights keep the kit they were tuned with (the same rule as Free Battle-only specials).
- **The impact shot holds on the point of impact** rather than following the target, so what rises out of the blow stays in frame.

// extras only: forms: ['ssj3'], freeUnlock: '<mission id>', freeOnly: true, ultimate: true

### 8.10 Slice 5 (2026-10-16): Majin Vegeta's Free Battle place and Final Impact

**Decision on the Majin form outside the story (brief Phase B step 6), documented:** the form stays `storyLocked` — the transform button never reaches it. Its place in `vegeta.js`'s form list is after Super Saiyan Blue, so opening it to the button would make Blue → Majin the next transformation; reordering the list would move form indices that missions' `maxForm` rules and saved starting forms use. Outside the story, Majin Vegeta is played from **Free Battle / Training → Starting Form: Majin**, listed once *The Mark* is cleared (`form.freeStart: 'buu_majin'`). **Final Impact** is earned for Free Battle by clearing *The Mark* (`buu_majin`, the mission that gives the form) — the same rule as Raditz and Nappa Rush.

**Found while checking this, then fixed at the owner's request (same day):** the Starting Form row listed every form that was not `storyOnly`, earned or not (a new save could start as Mastered Ultra Instinct Goku). It now follows the same rule as the transform button (`SL.formOpenFree` / `SL.formStartFree`, `form.freeStart` on Majin; the owner made the other two story-locked forms with no `freeUnlock`, Super Vegeta and Fused Piccolo, story-only). STATUS → *Free Battle / Training Starting Form follows the form unlocks*.

**Final Impact** (`vegeta.js` extra `finalImpact`; a game-original name for Majin Vegeta's close work, not a named technique): Majin form only, Free Battle / Training only, earned by *The Mark*, 50 ki. Hunched wind-up, a head-first charge, a knee into the stomach, a held moment with the hand raised at the chest and ki gathering in it, then a point-blank blast that goes through him and throws him away. Engine: a rush step `hits[i].aim: { color, hand }` — no blow; he holds the step's pose while ki gathers in that hand; fires the cine event `aim`; a `ki` step may add `pierce` (a streak through the target). New shot `aim` (`aimShot`: on the side of the raised hand, a little ahead of him — his face, the hand, the target beyond).

**Fixed while checking it (shared):** the skill wheel could never select some lower-left slots by pointing (keyboard, stick or mouse) — `RadialMenu.pick` compared an `atan2` angle (−π..π) with slot angles (0..2π) and folded the gap only once, so a gap over a full turn came out negative and always won. A five-entry wheel (Majin Vegeta's) could not reach slot 3 — Final Impact's — a six-entry wheel slot 4, an eight-entry wheel slots 5 and 6. One line in `radial.js`; every character's wheel benefits.

**Left open:** the blast's brightest frames (about three ticks) wash the frame white — the shared hit light (`vfx.impact` level ≥ 3 adds a strong point light) plus the explosion's; the move has no slow motion on the blast so those frames are not held. The shared bright-flash task (8.13) is where it gets fixed.

**CPU use of the long rushes (inspected, not changed).** A CPU Vegito Blue in Free Battle can choose either one: the shared `wheelRush` AI picks any earned wheel rush, outside the story only. Its rush has no cinematic shots and no impact frame. **Escape from long rushes — proposal only (not built; the owner's call).** Today the held state has no escape, except Dabura's stone, which is mashed. The engine already has a **burst escape** out of hitstun (`_burstEscape`: costs `R.COST.burstEscape` stamina, vanishes 7 m away, 16 invulnerable frames). Options:
1. **Burst out at the vanishes** — while held by a rush with `h.to` or `h.move` steps, a dodge pressed in that step's frames runs the existing burst. The window is readable (the attacker has just vanished), costs stamina, and adds no new rule or UI. Only long Free Battle rushes would carry the flag (e.g. `sp.escape: 'vanish'`). Story `held` holds stay untouched, because they are not rush-held.
2. **Mash meter** like the stone (strikes shorten the hold) — simple, but a rush ends early whenever mashed, which cuts the choreography short.
3. **No escape; fairness through cost** — keep the ki cost and add a cooldown or a CPU restriction; nothing to learn.

Recommendation: option 1, limited to rushes over ~3 s, if the owner wants an escape at all. It reuses the burst, keeps the choreography whole when not answered, and leaves the story alone.
